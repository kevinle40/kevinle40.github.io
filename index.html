Sheet 01

Two-Stage Planetary Gearbox for a Robotic Arm

The arm's stock motor had plenty of speed but not nearly enough torque at the wrist. I designed a compact two-stage planetary reduction to fit the existing housing envelope, modeling gear mesh and bearing loads in Excel before cutting anything, then machined and assembled a working prototype on the shop's manual mill.

The final unit delivered a 25:1 reduction inside a housing 12mm shorter than the original budget, and held under 0.05mm of backlash after 40 hours of continuous run testing.

RoleSole designer & machinist
ToolsSolidWorks, MATLAB
Materials6061-T6, C360 brass

Sheet 02

Quick-Release Bracket for a Wind Tunnel Test Fixture

Our team's fixture required a full re-bolt between every model swap, which was eating hours out of a limited tunnel booking. I redesigned the mounting interface around a spring-loaded quick-release pin, sized for the fixture's vibration and load profile using a quick FEA check in Fusion 360.

Swap time dropped from roughly 20 minutes to under 90 seconds, and the bracket has run through three semesters of testing with no failures.

RoleDesign lead, 3-person team
ToolsFusion 360, FEA
Materials4140 steel
